About Me

Meng Liu is an Assistant Professor of HENU-100 Talents Program at Henan University. He received the Ph.D. degree from National University of Defense Technology supervised by Prof. Xinwang Liu, and was also a Joint Student at National University of Singapore supervised by Prof. Beng Chin OOI. His research interests include graph neural network, multi-modal, and clustering. He has published more than 30 papers including TPAMI, AdvSci, TKDE, and NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, with 1 ESI Hot Paper, 3 ESI Highly Cited Papers, and 1500 citations. He received awards such as Best Paper of 2024 China Computational Power Conference, 2025 DAAD AInet Fellowship, Youth Outstanding Paper Shortlist of WAIC 2025, Best Student Paper of CCHI 2023, Excellent Paper of GHMIG 2025, etc. He is on the Editorial Board of Information Processing & Management, Scientific Reports, and Youth Editorial Board of Engineered Science, INSC. He also serves as Area Chair for ICLR, ICASSP, COLM, and reviewer for TPAMI, TKDE, AIJ, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, etc.

Email: mengliuedu@163.com / mengliu@henu.edu.cn
