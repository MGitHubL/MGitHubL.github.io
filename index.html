About Me

Meng Liu is a Hundred Talents Program Researcher at Henan University. He received the Ph.D. degree from National University of Defense Technology supervised by Prof. Xinwang Liu, and was also a Joint Student at National University of Singapore supervised by Prof. Beng Chin OOI. His research interests include graph neural network, multi-modal, and clustering. He has published more than 30 papers including TPAMI, AdvSci, TKDE, and ICLR, ICML, CVPR, with 1 ESI Hot Paper, 3 ESI Highly Cited Papers, and 1700 citations. He received awards such as Best Paper of China Computational Power Conference, DAAD AInet Fellowship, Outstanding Young Scholar of AAIEC, etc. He serves as an Associate Editor of Pattern Recognition, an Editorial Board Member of IP&M, and an Area Chair for ICLR, NeurIPS, etc.

Email: mengliuedu@163.com / mengliu@henu.edu.cn
